## 2. Bright room: contrast under screen glare

Effective contrast = (Y₁·L + R) / (Y₂·L + R), with the screen at **L = 800 nits** and R = luminance reflected off the glass (≈ 5 % of ambient illuminance ÷ π). Below about 3:1, numbers start to wash out.

| Ambient | R (nits) | Chalk: score on bg | Whiteboard: score on bg |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living room, evening (150 lx) | 2 | 53.1:1 | 77.9:1 |
| Office / kitchen (500 lx) | 8 | 37.2:1 | 49.1:1 |
| Bright room by a window (2 000 lx) | 32 | 16.7:1 | 19.4:1 |
| Direct sun through glass (10 000 lx) | 159 | 4.9:1 | 5.3:1 |

The ratios are similar, but dark-on-light (positive polarity) reads better in bright surroundings: the pupil narrows and bright text on dark halates, especially with astigmatism. **Whiteboard is the bright-room theme.** Safari exposes no ambient-light sensor, so the app follows the system appearance and adds a one-tap *Bright room* switch in the in-game menu.

## 4. Size from the oche (2.4 m) and in the hand (0.5 m)

Digit height = font size × digit-height ratio, measured in the browser (Barlow Condensed 800: 0.714 em; Atkinson Hyperlegible 700: 0.68 em). Angle = visual angle at the role's viewing distance. **Targets: ≥ 16′ minimum, ≥ 20′ preferred** (ISO 9241-303 / ANSI-HFES 100 for character height). For reference, a 20/20 letter is 5′.

Each role states the distance it is read from: **2.4 m** (the oche) or **0.35 m** (phone in hand). Roles marked *secondary* have a lower floor of 10′ (2× a 20/20 letter) because a larger number carries the same information, e.g. the turn total above the chips.
