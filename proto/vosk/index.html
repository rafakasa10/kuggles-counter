2 · Push-to-talk: 36 phrases

Tap the mic, say the phrase, then stop talking. It stops on its own after 1.2 s of silence. A match moves on by itself.

3 · Hands-free

The mic stays open with no button. Prop the phone up by the board and step back to the oche (about 2.4 m). Say each turn the way you would in a game; pauses between darts are fine. Leave it running for 15 minutes or more for the endurance test.

“Test speech output” is how the app will work: it stops listening while it speaks. The “mic left open” version checks whether the phone's echo cancellation alone would stop the recogniser from hearing its own voice.

How did “One hundred and eighty” sound?

Chatter test: talk normally (or leave the TV on) for 60 s without saying darts. Anything it picks up counts as a false trigger.
